Layers

    Drag layers to reorder them. Drop on the middle of a group or hover container to put a layer inside it, or on empty space to move it out. The top of the list is drawn in front.

    Shortcuts

    Arrows nudge (Shift = 10px). Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo. Ctrl+C / V copy and paste. Ctrl+D duplicate. Ctrl+G groups, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups. Alt+click selects the container of what you click. Delete removes (with everything inside a group). Esc deselects. Ctrl+S saves the project.

    Export

    1. Save as plugins/VeloxGUI/guis/my-gui.json
    2. Upload images to plugins/VeloxGUI/images/
    3. Run /vgui reload, look at a wall, run /vgui place my-gui
